# Method

The certificate checks Algorithm 3's soundness first. Whole instance columns are assigned once, encountered instances remain the initial segment through `J`, and `J` is unbounded whether rounds halt or one round issues infinitely many queries. Thus `F` is total recursive. Universal columns make every indexed support infinite.

The minimum queue policy enumerates `Q_infinity`. Every output sequence satisfies exactly one of two cases. Infinitely many non-`h1` outputs release every trap, make the queue equal `supp(h1)`, and receive exclusive diagonal points outside `h1`. Finitely many leave a final trap target whose support equals the queue; eventual `h1` includes the omitted trap point. Either route forces infinitely many properness errors.
