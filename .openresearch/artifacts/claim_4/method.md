# Method

The proof certificate checks both sides. In the standard setting, the largest marker stabilizes to the unique padding index `b`, after which the source's extreme-integer generator `G^b` handles the relevant half-line subclass. The all-marker target is handled separately.

For replay hardness, a fresh marker `star^z` is replayed while the adversary runs every phase `n`. Counterfactually, a nonterminating phase is a legal enumeration for an `H1^(z-1)` target and therefore must force a fresh integer above `J_(n-1)`, proving phase termination. The actual target contains all integers below `z`, omits `z`, has markers through `z`, and withholds every `J_n`; hence infinitely many outputs are invalid.
