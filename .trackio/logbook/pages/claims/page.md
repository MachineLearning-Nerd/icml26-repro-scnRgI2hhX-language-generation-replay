## Anchored claims

1. Uniform generation is equivalent with and without replay at identical sample complexity.
2. Replay separates countable non-uniform generation.
3. Witness Protection generates countable classes in the limit under replay using membership queries.
4. Replay separates in-the-limit generation for an uncountable class.
5. Membership queries alone cannot universally support proper in-the-limit generation.
6. A four-hypothesis finite class is impossible to properly generate in the limit with replay.
